Focus Flow privacy

A private app for a family and friends. Last updated November 2026.

What is stored

Your name and email, your password (stored scrambled, never readable by anyone), and what you put into the app: tasks, goals, settings, check-ins and plans. If you turn on phone alerts, your phone's alert address is stored so reminders can reach it.

Who can see it

  • Your tasks, goals, settings and check-ins are private to you. Other people in the app cannot read them.
  • Your name is visible to other signed-in people, so they can invite you and see who is in a plan.
  • A shared plan, its people and its tasks are visible only to the people in that plan. Someone you invite sees the plan's title until they accept.
  • The person who runs this copy of Focus Flow has technical access to the database, as the host of any app does.

Who else handles it

Your data is held by Supabase (database and sign-in). The app page is served by GitHub Pages. Email is sent through Gmail. Phone alerts go through your phone maker's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla). Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Crash reports

If the app hits an error, a short technical report (the error message, the page and your browser type) is sent so it can be fixed. It does not include your tasks.

Backups

A copy of everything is made each night and kept for 14 days, in case something goes wrong. Deleted data may remain in those copies until they expire.

Your choices

  • Turn phone alerts and emails off any time in Settings.
  • Copy your data: Settings, Your account, Show backup text.
  • Delete everything: Settings, Your account, Delete my account. This removes your data, the plans you own, your place in other plans and your sign-in.
